Features

Everything you need in one focused place

Built for students who know where they want to go but not where to start.

01

Student profile

Tell us your degree, year, preferred language, current level and how many hours you can study each day.

02

Six career paths

Frontend, Backend, Full-Stack, Python, Java and AI/ML, each with its own skills, projects and interview topics.

03

Matched to your level

Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced changes how long each topic takes and which projects you are asked to build.

04

Personalized roadmap

Ordered skills, practice tasks, projects, free resources and interview topics, with a duration that follows your daily study hours.

05

Progress dashboard

Tick off tasks, see completed and remaining work and watch your percentage grow. Progress survives a refresh.

06

Private by design

No account, no server, no tracking. Your data stays in your own browser's LocalStorage and you can erase it anytime.

About

About SkillPath

SkillPath is a final project designed and built by Rachabattuni Divya. It is a front-end-only web app made with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with no frameworks, paid services or backend.

The personalization is transparent and rule-based, not artificial intelligence. Your level decides whether foundation topics become quick reviews and which projects are recommended, and your daily study hours decide the schedule. The roadmap page lists exactly which rules were applied to you.

What it is not

  • It does not guarantee a job offer.
  • Your level is what you tell us. SkillPath does not test you.
  • Durations are estimates for a beginner studying consistently.
  • External learning links are free resources chosen by hand; availability can change.
